How I work

Learning moves forward when people can try it.

Start with the audience and their real context. Then make space to learn, practice, and connect the experience to what happens next at work.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Begin with the work

Get clear on the audience, current challenges, and what the team wants to be able to do.

02 / LEARN

Make it relevant

Shape examples and learning activities around the people, tools, products, and context involved.

03 / PRACTICE

Build confidence

Use guided practice and reflection to help people explore how learning may transfer to their work.
